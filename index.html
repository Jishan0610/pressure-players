2 · What counts as pressure

The top 20% of balls by stakes or adversity

Stakes: how much this ball matters (close and late). Adversity: how much trouble the side is in. Pressure = the higher of the two; the top 20% of balls count.

Why 20%? It is the strictest line where a player's past pressure record still predicts their future one.
